Voxclunibus is a small print-on-demand t-shirt brand built around one simple idea: some thoughts are too real to keep to yourself. We make shirts for people who are done pretending — done pretending to enjoy Mondays, done pretending the recliner isn't their happy place, done pretending they have it together.

The name

Voxclunibus is fake Latin. It doesn't mean anything. Which, honestly, feels appropriate for a brand about shirts that say things you probably shouldn't say out loud. We liked the sound of it. That's it.

How it works

Every shirt is printed on demand by our partner Printful the moment you order it. No warehouse. No overstock. No unsold boxes of shirts sitting in someone's garage. Your order goes straight from press to postbox.

We use Gildan 64000 — 100% ring-spun cotton, pre-shrunk, soft, and built to actually last. The print is direct-to-garment (DTG), which means it's durable when washed properly (inside-out, cold water, no tumble dry).
