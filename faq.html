FAQ

Orders & Shipping

How long does shipping take?
Your shirt is printed within 2–5 business days, then shipped. Delivery takes 3–7 additional business days within Europe and the US. Total: roughly 5–12 business days from order to doorstep.
Do you ship worldwide?
Yes! We ship to most countries via our partner Printful. Shipping rates are calculated at checkout based on your location.
Will I get a tracking number?
Yes. Once your order ships, you'll receive an email with a tracking link so you can follow your package.
Can I change or cancel my order?
Because shirts are printed on demand, orders go to production quickly. If you need to change or cancel, email us at support@voxclunibus.com as soon as possible. We'll do our best, but can't guarantee changes once printing has started.

Returns & Refunds

What if my shirt is defective or wrong?
We'll replace it or refund you immediately. Just send a photo to support@voxclunibus.com within 14 days of receiving your order.
Can I return if I just changed my mind?
EU/EEA customers have a 14-day right of withdrawal. The shirt must be unworn and in original condition. Return shipping is at your cost. Email us to start the process.

Sizing

What shirt do you use?

Products

Will the print fade?
Our shirts use direct-to-garment (DTG) printing which is durable when cared for properly. Wash inside-out in cold water and avoid tumble drying to keep the print looking sharp.
Are the colours accurate to the photos?
We do our best to show accurate product photos. Screen colours vary, and print colours can differ slightly from what you see on screen. If you're unsatisfied with the print quality, contact us and we'll sort it out.

Still have questions?

Email us at support@voxclunibus.com — we typically reply within 1–2 business days.
