Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Voxclunibus ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting your personal data. This policy explains what we collect, why, and how we use it — in plain language.

1. Who we are

Voxclunibus is an online store selling printed-on-demand t-shirts. We operate at voxclunibus.com. For questions about your data, contact us at support@voxclunibus.com.

2. What data we collect

When you place an order, we collect:

  • Your name and delivery address
  • Your email address
  • Payment information (processed securely by Stripe — we never see your card number)

If you sign up for our newsletter, we collect your email address only.

We do not collect any data when you just browse the store.

3. Why we collect it

  • To fulfill your order — we pass your name and address to Printful, our print and shipping partner, so they can send your t-shirt.
  • To send order confirmations — we email you a receipt and shipping updates via Stripe.
  • To notify you of new products — only if you signed up for our mailing list. You can unsubscribe at any time.

4. Who we share your data with

We share your data with the following third parties, only as necessary to fulfill your order:

  • Stripe — payment processing. Stripe Privacy Policy
  • Printful — print and shipping. Printful Privacy Policy
  • Vercel — website hosting. Your IP address may be logged by Vercel's servers.

We do not sell your data to anyone. Ever.

5. How long we keep your data

Order data is retained for up to 5 years for accounting and legal purposes, as required by Norwegian law. Newsletter subscriptions are kept until you unsubscribe.

6. Your rights (GDPR)

If you are in the EU or Norway, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your data
  • Object to how we use your data
  • Request a copy of your data in a portable format

To exercise these rights, email us at support@voxclunibus.com. We will respond within 30 days.

7. Cookies

We use minimal cookies. Stripe may set cookies for fraud prevention purposes. We do not use tracking or advertising cookies.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy occasionally. We will post the updated version on this page with a new date. Significant changes will be communicated by email if we have your address.

9. Contact

Questions? Email us: support@voxclunibus.com
