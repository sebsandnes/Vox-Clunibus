Returns & Refunds

Last updated: May 2025

We want you to love your shirt. If something's wrong, we'll make it right.

Defective or wrong item

If your shirt arrives damaged, with a printing error, or is the wrong item — we will replace it or refund you fully, no questions asked. Email us within 14 days of delivery with a photo of the issue: support@voxclunibus.com

Right of withdrawal (EU/EEA)

EU and EEA customers have the right to return any item within 14 days of receiving it, without giving a reason. The item must be unworn, unwashed, and in its original condition.

To start a return, email us at support@voxclunibus.com with your order number. We will send you return instructions.

Return shipping costs are the customer's responsibility unless the item is defective. Refunds are processed within 14 days of receiving the returned item.

Wrong size?

Because our shirts are printed on demand, we cannot exchange sizes directly. If you ordered the wrong size, return the unworn item and place a new order in the correct size.

How to contact us

Email: support@voxclunibus.com

We typically respond within 1–2 business days.
